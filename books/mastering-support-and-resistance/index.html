About this book

Mastering Support & Resistance is a visual guide to reading support and resistance as behavioral zones rather than perfect single-price lines. It develops demand and supply zones, liquidity references, role reversal after a breakout, bounce setups, breakout-and-retest structures, fake-breakout traps, wick-versus-body interpretation, repeated closes, round-number reactions, invalidation, position sizing, and journaling. The visual framework emphasizes confirmation and risk control instead of assuming that any marked level must hold.

What you will learn

  • Draw support and resistance as reaction zones rather than demanding exact-price precision.
  • Read rejection wicks, closes, repeated acceptance, and role reversal after breakouts.
  • Differentiate bounce, breakout-retest, and fake-breakout scenarios.
  • Use round numbers and liquidity references as context rather than automatic signals.
  • Define confirmation, invalidation, position size, and risk before committing capital.
