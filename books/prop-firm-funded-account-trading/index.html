About this book

Prop Firm / Funded Account Trading treats the prop-firm rulebook as a second market. Price determines whether a trade is working; the rules determine whether the trader is still allowed to operate. The book therefore starts with evaluations, maximum and daily loss limits, trailing drawdown, profit targets, funded-account constraints and position sizing—not with a shortcut for passing a challenge. Its central idea is that the headline account balance is less important than the distance to the rule-defined failure boundary.

The playbook then expands into risk ladders, consecutive-loss control, correlation exposure, strategy/firm fit, session architecture and high-impact news. Later chapters address the psychological pressures of evaluations, the shift from pass mode to survival mode, payout buffers, withdrawing versus compounding, scaling, dashboards and journaling. Due diligence, rulebook analysis, red flags and a prop-firm scorecard are followed by case studies and operational tools including pre-market checks, shutdown and recovery protocols, risk planners, position-size worksheets, payout calculations and weekly review.

What you will learn

  • Translate a prop-firm rulebook into measurable failure boundaries and risk variables.
  • Treat drawdown and daily loss limits as the effective operating capital of the account.
  • Size positions inside funded-account constraints and adjust risk after losses, gains and rule changes.
  • Control consecutive-loss and correlation risk before they combine into a single account-level failure.
  • Decide whether a strategy, pace and session structure actually fit a specific firm’s rules.
  • Shift from evaluation behavior to funded-account survival and build a buffer before payout decisions.
  • Perform due diligence, identify red flags and compare firms through a structured scorecard.
  • Use pre-market checks, shutdown protocols, recovery plans, dashboards and journals as an operating system rather than relying on memory.
