Description publique originale du livre

anglais

The Monster in the Mirror explores how fear, pain, rumor, and judgment can flatten another person into a frightening symbol. It does not ask readers to deny danger or abandon boundaries; it asks them to separate fact from fear, discernment from demonization, and protection from projection. Fourteen chapters and a twenty-one-day mirror practice examine borrowed monsters, gossip, hidden wounds, the pause before judgment, one’s own shadow, and the possibility of restoring a human face without surrendering clarity.

Objectifs d'apprentissage publiés

anglais

  • Separate observable danger from stories amplified by fear and projection.
  • Use discernment without reducing a whole person to a single frightening label.
  • Recognize borrowed fears, gossip, and unresolved pain in judgment.
  • Examine one's own shadow before assigning all darkness to others.
  • Hold boundaries and clarity while restoring a more human view of reality.
