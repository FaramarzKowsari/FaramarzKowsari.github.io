About this book

Premium & Discount Zones turns the familiar upper-half/lower-half range idea into a complete decision framework. The book begins with the dealing range and its 50% equilibrium, then asks the harder question: which swing high and swing low deserve to define that range? From there it connects relative location to market structure, internal and external liquidity, nested ranges, Fibonacci retracement, Fair Value Gaps, order blocks, breakers and liquidity sweeps.

The core message is that location is not an entry signal. Discount can improve the location of a bullish idea and premium can improve the location of a bearish idea, but only when the range is defensible and the market supplies evidence. Multi-timeframe context, sessions, volatility, invalidation, target geometry, risk-to-reward, case studies and journaling are used to turn the map into a repeatable process rather than a colored rectangle.

What you will learn

  • Define a defensible dealing range before using premium, discount or equilibrium.
  • Treat the 50% line as a relative-location reference rather than a universal fair-value claim.
  • Choose meaningful swing highs and lows without moving the range to rescue a losing thesis.
  • Connect premium and discount to market structure, liquidity and the path price took into the area.
  • Use FVGs, order blocks, breakers and sweeps as contextual evidence rather than automatic entries.
  • Separate higher-timeframe thesis invalidation from lower-timeframe execution invalidation.
  • Evaluate targets and reward space before taking a trade, not after entry.
  • Build a journalable decision framework that can end with trade, wait or no trade.
