About this book

Crypto Liquidation Heatmaps separates three datasets traders often confuse: realized liquidation events, projected model-based liquidation vulnerability, and displayed order-book liquidity. The book explains how liquidation works, how projected maps are estimated from incomplete public information, why different providers can disagree, and why a bright cluster is not automatically a price magnet.

The framework then combines liquidation maps with open interest, funding, volume, CVD and market structure. It studies long and short liquidations, cascades, squeezes, sweeps, continuation, opposing clusters and no-trade conditions. Position sizing and stop placement are treated conservatively because leveraged crypto can reprice faster than a static heatmap suggests, and published-event case studies distinguish what was observable live from what became known afterward.

What you will learn

  • Distinguish realized liquidations, projected liquidation levels and order-book liquidity.
  • Understand how projected heatmaps infer vulnerability when individual account liquidation prices are not public.
  • Judge cluster distance, density and model stability without treating heatmaps as deterministic magnets.
  • Combine liquidation information with open interest, funding, volume, CVD and market structure.
  • Differentiate liquidation sweeps, cascades, squeezes and ordinary breakouts.
  • Use rule-based reversal, cascade-continuation and opposing-cluster setups.
  • Place stops and size positions without using liquidation itself as the planned risk boundary.
  • Evaluate provider limits, exchange coverage, model assumptions and historical event reconstructions critically.
