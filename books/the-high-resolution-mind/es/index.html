Descripción pública original del libro

inglés

The High-Resolution Mind examines very high cognitive ability without turning IQ into a measure of human worth or a mythology of genius. It explores the limits of IQ scores, cognitive speed and depth, asynchronous development, solitude, overthinking, emotional intensity, sensory sensitivity, social masking, understimulation, perfectionism, intellectual humility, memory, love, work, authority, twice-exceptionality, darker failure modes, and the ethics of designing a life around unusual cognitive needs. Research claims are framed with attention to sampling and generalization limits.

Objetivos de aprendizaje publicados

inglés

  • Separate measured IQ from human value and from the full range of cognitive ability.
  • Recognize how high cognitive intensity may interact with solitude, understimulation, and overthinking.
  • Understand emotional intensity, sensory sensitivity, and the cost of social masking.
  • Develop healthier approaches to perfectionism, work, authority, and relationships.
  • Read research on gifted populations with attention to sample limitations and uncertainty.
