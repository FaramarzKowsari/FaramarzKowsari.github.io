About this book

VWAP Trading presents Volume Weighted Average Price as a dynamic benchmark for organizing price and volume, not as a magical support-and-resistance line or a stand-alone trading signal. The book develops a context-first way to read where price is trading relative to volume-weighted fair value, how that relationship changes through a session, and why the same touch, cross or distance from VWAP can mean very different things in a balanced auction, a directional trend or a transition between regimes. Session VWAP is treated as an evolving reference that must be interpreted together with price behavior, participation, volatility, market structure and execution conditions.

The framework then expands from Session VWAP into Anchored VWAP and deviation bands. Anchors are tied to meaningful events or structural reference points rather than chosen arbitrarily, allowing price to be compared with volume-weighted value since a specific market event. Deviation bands are used as contextual measures of distance and dispersion rather than mechanical overbought-or-oversold triggers. The book examines the open relative to VWAP, VWAP slope and interaction, reclaim and rejection structures, acceptance versus brief line crossings, continuation and mean-reversion conditions, session profiles, multi-timeframe context, and the role of volume and volatility in deciding whether a VWAP observation is actionable or merely descriptive.

Execution and research are central to the book. Setup definitions connect context, location, trigger, invalidation and target logic, while risk chapters address sizing, stop logic and the practical consequences of slippage and execution quality. Backtesting guidance emphasizes predefined rules, realistic assumptions, adequate samples and protection against look-ahead bias, cherry-picking and subjective anchor selection. Dedicated failure-mode chapters show why VWAP can become misleading when session boundaries, regime, volume context or anchors are poorly defined, and the final playbook material turns the concepts into explicit setup criteria, no-trade filters, review metrics and a repeatable decision process rather than a collection of isolated chart patterns.

A useful benchmark is not an instruction to buy every touch.

VWAP is widely recognized as an execution benchmark, but the fact that professional traders use it does not establish that every touch reveals hidden institutional intent. This book draws a necessary line between measurement and interpretation: understand the calculation, choose the correct session definition or anchor, then ask what price and volume are actually doing relative to the reference.

The 25-chapter journey advances from VWAP mathematics to Session VWAP, Anchored VWAP, multi-anchor readings and standard-deviation bands; then explores opening auctions, reclaims, rejections, trends and mean reversion. It closes with execution risk, market regimes, backtesting, failure modes, historical-data case studies and a personal playbook that can be reviewed without hindsight.

Three mistakes the book helps you recognize

Two traders, two different session VWAPs

A benchmark changes with its starting point and session definition. Before comparing two charts, make the input conventions explicit.

A band looks stretched but trend conditions persist

Being far from VWAP does not force a reversal. Deviation bands describe variation; price may continue moving in a strongly directional market.

The reclaim works on the chart, but not after costs

A visual reaction can be untradable once order type, fees, spreads, adverse fills and planned risk are included.

Before using the next chart: six practical questions

  1. What dataset, interval, session definition and range or anchor are in use?
  2. What measurement is objectively known, and what is an interpretation?
  3. What other market state could make the apparent pattern misleading?
  4. What observation would invalidate the hypothesis?
  5. Are execution costs and risk acceptable for the planned trade?
  6. Could this setup be tested without selecting winners after the fact?

What readers ask about the book

What is VWAP?

Volume-Weighted Average Price is a benchmark based on traded price and volume over a defined interval. The interval and data quality determine the calculation.

What is the difference between Session VWAP and Anchored VWAP?

Session VWAP resets according to a defined market session, while Anchored VWAP begins at a selected event or timestamp. Different anchors can tell different stories.

Are standard-deviation bands reliable reversal signals?

No. The source treats them as descriptive statistics. A stretched reading alone is not a trading recommendation.

THE MARKET-MICROSTRUCTURE DESK · VISUAL ANALYSIS 01

VWAP Is Not a Price Level. It Is a Record of Participation.

Volume-weighted price, trade volume and the difference between a benchmark and a signal

Volume-Weighted Average Price answers a narrower, more useful question than a typical crossover signal: where did trading take place, on average, when the size of each trade is counted? For bar data, a common approximation is cumulative typical price multiplied by volume, divided by cumulative volume. A 10,000-share bar should affect that benchmark more than a 100-share bar, even if both closes move the same distance.

This makes VWAP valuable as a description of participation, not a guarantee that buyers will defend the line. The distance between the last price and the running benchmark can help organize intraday context. Yet above VWAP is not automatically bullish, and below VWAP is not automatically bearish: session selection, price path, market liquidity and the distribution of trades all matter.

The practical research question is whether a specific price response near VWAP repeats under comparable conditions. The book VWAP Trading by Faramarz Kowsari develops that distinction between what the indicator measures and what a trader is tempted to infer.

SESSION STRUCTURE REPORT · VISUAL ANALYSIS 02

A Reset Can Change the Story Without Changing a Single Trade.

Session VWAP, opening context and reset conventions

A session VWAP begins again at a specified market boundary. That choice sounds technical until two analysts examine identical price data and produce different VWAP lines. Equities may emphasize regular trading hours, while futures trade across extended sessions; the meaning of an overnight reading depends on the market and the platform's reset setting.

At the open, cumulative volume is still small, so a new burst of trades can reposition the benchmark quickly. Later in the day, accumulated participation gives VWAP more inertia. The same five-tick move can therefore have very different significance at 09:35 and 15:45.

A disciplined comparison must record instrument, exchange timezone, session hours, volume source and calculation input before judging reclaims or deviations. VWAP Trading includes session definitions and reproducibility checklists for turning attractive chart stories into testable observations.

ANCHORED ANALYSIS · VISUAL ANALYSIS 03

Anchored VWAP Gives Price a Memory. The Anchor Must Earn Its Place.

Anchored VWAP around events, structural pivots and multiple reference points

Anchored VWAP changes the question from 'Where is today's weighted price?' to 'Where has volume-weighted trading occurred since this particular event?' A breakout, earnings announcement, important swing or objectively defined gap can justify a starting point when it represents a change in market participation.

The trap is retrospective anchor shopping. If an analyst drags the start point until a curve perfectly touches a later reversal, the picture may be persuasive and the method untestable. Define candidate anchors in advance, document the event and test whether the rule survives different samples.

Two independently defensible anchors can converge near the same price. That intersection may deserve attention because participants with different holding periods are viewing nearby benchmarks; it does not reveal their positions. The book explains single- and multi-anchored VWAP as hypotheses that must be checked against actual price response and risk.

SETUP OBSERVATORY · VISUAL ANALYSIS 04

Crossing VWAP Is Easy. Proving Acceptance Is Harder.

Reclaims, rejections, retests and failed breakouts

A reclaim is more informative when price recovers VWAP, holds above it and establishes a plausible structural low, rather than merely printing one candle across the line. The reverse logic applies to a rejection: a failed test should be read together with context, volatility, nearby liquidity and the available distance to a target.

False crossovers proliferate when price rotates tightly around fair value. A chart can record several apparent signals while producing no usable expectancy after spread and slippage. Traders should distinguish a temporary crossing from sustained acceptance by predefining what confirmation looks like and how long it must persist.

Useful setup notes include market regime, directional bias, trigger, invalidation, target and a reason to avoid the trade. The dedicated reclaim and rejection chapters of Faramarz Kowsari's VWAP Trading develop precisely that evidence-first approach.

DEVIATION WATCH · VISUAL ANALYSIS 05

Far From VWAP Does Not Mean Price Must Come Back.

Standard-deviation bands, trend strength and conditional mean reversion

VWAP bands often mark an estimated dispersion around a volume-weighted center. They are useful for expressing distance, but a statistical band is not a mechanical ceiling. Strong buying can keep price extended for much of a session while an attempted short based only on a high-band touch accumulates losses.

Mean reversion becomes a more defensible hypothesis in a two-sided, rotational market where price repeatedly travels through the weighted center and responsive participation appears at extremes. It becomes a weaker hypothesis when volume, price structure and persistent directional acceptance indicate repricing.

Ask what the band calculation actually uses, whether it resets with the session and whether volatility is changing. The book's treatment of deviation bands emphasizes conditions, failures and trade construction rather than automatic 'overbought' and 'oversold' labels.

REGIME INTELLIGENCE · VISUAL ANALYSIS 06

The Same VWAP Can Support Opposite Trades in Different Markets.

Balanced auctions, directional sessions, slope and continuation

Consider two sessions. In one, price repeatedly crosses a flat VWAP while trading volume spreads across a broad range. In the other, VWAP slopes steadily and price spends most of its time on one side. A trader who uses identical entry rules in both markets is testing two different mechanisms under one label.

A regime filter should be observable before the outcome: VWAP slope over a defined interval, the proportion of time spent on each side, opening-range behavior, volatility relative to recent sessions and the shape of recent structure. None is infallible, but they can make a hypothesis falsifiable.

The key is resisting the urge to label the day only after the profitable move is obvious. VWAP Trading connects price-volume interpretation to market-state classification, then asks how trade logic changes in rotation, continuation or transition.

CROSS-TIMEFRAME BRIEFING · VISUAL ANALYSIS 07

The Opening Hour and Higher-Timeframe Anchors Do Not Always Agree.

Opening auction, volume profile and multi-timeframe VWAP

Opening activity can establish reference points for the rest of the day, but the first reaction around VWAP may be noisy. Compare the developing average with the opening range, gap context and the location of the prior session's volume concentration before assigning directional significance.

An anchored VWAP from a major event may sit far from today's session line. That is not an error; each calculation includes a different history. Rather than adding every timeframe to the screen, assign a specific job to each benchmark: current intraday participation, post-event positioning or a longer structural reference.

Volume Profile adds a separate dimension by describing how much volume was transacted at different prices, rather than collapsing trading into a single weighted average. A crowded high-volume node and a session VWAP may align, but the relationship still needs interpretation. The book treats these tools as complementary measurements, not competing oracles.

EXECUTION & RESEARCH · VISUAL ANALYSIS 08

A Beautiful VWAP Backtest Can Disappear at the Fill.

Slippage, transaction costs, risk sizing and honest testing

An attractive chart commonly ignores the difference between a visible price and an executable fill. Spread, slippage, fees, order size and market impact all affect a VWAP idea. A breakout entry at a fast-moving price may fill well beyond the expected level, changing both the reward-to-risk ratio and the stop distance.

A credible test fixes the VWAP reset or anchor rule, market and timeframe, confirmation threshold, exit and position sizing before looking at outcomes. It separates in-sample development from out-of-sample evaluation and includes enough trades to estimate uncertainty. Selecting only the clearest screenshots is not a substitute for a full sample.

Faramarz Kowsari's VWAP Trading closes the loop with execution, backtesting, failure modes, case studies and a trading-journal template. The point is not to find a perfect line; it is to build a decision that can be explained, repeated and reviewed when it fails.

What you will learn

  • Understand what VWAP measures and why volume weighting makes it different from a conventional moving average.
  • Use Session VWAP as an evolving benchmark whose meaning depends on session structure, price behavior, participation and regime.
  • Define session boundaries and reset conventions consistently so chart interpretation and backtesting refer to the same market event.
  • Select Anchored VWAP starting points from defensible events or structural references instead of arbitrary chart locations.
  • Interpret deviation bands as contextual measures of distance and dispersion rather than automatic overbought or oversold signals.
  • Classify the open and subsequent price behavior relative to VWAP, including slope, acceptance, rejection and changing session profiles.
  • Build VWAP reclaim and rejection setups that require context, confirmation, invalidation and realistic target space rather than a simple line cross.
  • Separate mean-reversion conditions from directional continuation conditions by combining VWAP with volume, volatility and market structure.
  • Use multi-timeframe and anchored references without overcrowding the chart or allowing conflicting benchmarks to replace a clear hypothesis.
  • Connect entry logic with position sizing, stop placement, execution quality, slippage and explicit no-trade conditions.
  • Backtest VWAP ideas with predefined rules, adequate samples and realistic execution while avoiding look-ahead bias, cherry-picking and subjective anchors.
