Descripción pública original del libro

inglés

ICT Silver Bullet narrows a broad SMC/ICT vocabulary into one time-bounded operating sequence. The clock is part of the setup: the book works with three New York local-time windows—03:00–04:00, 10:00–11:00 and 14:00–15:00—and emphasizes correct daylight-saving handling rather than hard-coded UTC offsets. Inside the window, the trader looks for a liquidity event, meaningful response, displacement, a relevant Fair Value Gap, retracement, defensible invalidation and rational objective. The New York AM window receives the deepest teaching focus, not as a claim of universally superior performance but as a controlled laboratory. Pre-window mapping, bullish and bearish models, no-trade branches, false positives, risk before reward, backtesting, case studies and a 20-session training plan make the method reviewable. Historical charts and schematic diagrams are explicitly distinguished so visual teaching is not confused with performance evidence.

Objetivos de aprendizaje publicados

inglés

  • Anchor Silver Bullet windows to New York local time and handle EST/EDT correctly.
  • Map liquidity and context before the one-hour window begins.
  • Require displacement and a relevant Fair Value Gap before treating a window as an execution opportunity.
  • Define entry, structural invalidation, target and risk without assuming the setup must appear.
  • Backtest and review Silver Bullet sequences, failures and no-trade sessions consistently.
