About this book

Inverse Fair Value Gaps (iFVG) begins where an ordinary Fair Value Gap fails. The book defines the original three-candle FVG geometry, then follows the sequence that can turn a violated imbalance into an inverse zone. A bullish iFVG is treated as a former bearish FVG reclaimed to the upside and evaluated from above; a bearish iFVG is the mirror case. The emphasis is on observable sequence rather than storytelling: what formed, what violated it, whether price accepted on the other side, and what would invalidate the new interpretation.

The framework then adds market structure, liquidity references, premium and discount location, dealing ranges and timeframe hierarchy. Higher timeframes are used for context while execution timeframes answer the narrower entry question. Three entry models, stops, targets, failure modes, backtesting, a complete process and eight case studies turn the visual pattern into a falsifiable trading rule rather than a colored rectangle.

What you will learn

  • Define bullish and bearish FVGs before deciding whether an inversion has occurred.
  • Identify the exact violation and acceptance sequence that can turn a prior imbalance into an inverse zone.
  • Connect iFVGs to market structure, liquidity, dealing-range location and premium or discount context.
  • Assign different jobs to higher, execution and lower timeframes without changing the controlling thesis after entry.
  • Use pre-defined entry models with explicit cancellation and invalidation conditions.
  • Separate zone quality from trade quality when nearby objectives, repeated tests, volatility or range location weaken the setup.
  • Backtest iFVGs with fixed definitions, failure examples and case-study comparison instead of hindsight.
