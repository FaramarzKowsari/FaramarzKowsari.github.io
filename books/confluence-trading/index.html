About this book

Confluence Trading treats confluence as a way to organize evidence, not a score that makes uncertainty disappear. The early chapters distinguish independent evidence from duplicate evidence and put market structure and location before indicators. Key levels, price action, support and resistance, Fibonacci, moving averages, momentum, volume and multiple timeframes are evaluated according to what new information they add rather than how many boxes they allow the trader to tick.

The later sections turn analysis into a trade thesis with a separate trigger, invalidation and risk budget. Conflicting evidence can reduce or cancel a setup, and risk is allowed to veto a visually beautiful chart. Trend pullback, breakout/retest, reversal and range-bound setups lead into hidden-right-edge exercises, a personal confluence playbook, backtest logs, pre-trade and post-trade worksheets and a visual appendix.

What you will actually study in Confluence Trading

What does the complete edition teach beyond a pattern name? The contents of Confluence Trading move from “One signal is not a system” through “The danger of confluence shopping” toward “Test the confluence, not the story.” These are not interchangeable trading buzzwords. Together they trace the questions a reader must answer when a convincing chart becomes a real decision with costs, uncertainty and the possibility of being wrong.

Instead of learning isolated labels, use the book's original progression to connect observation, context, risk and review. The chapter titles below come from its source contents, not from a generic list of trading keywords.

What you will learn

  • Distinguish independent evidence from multiple indicators that repeat the same information.
  • Put structure and location before confluence counting.
  • Use Fibonacci, moving averages, momentum and volume as context only when they add distinct evidence.
  • Separate thesis, trigger, invalidation and risk instead of treating confluence as an entry by itself.
  • Build and test a personal confluence playbook with worksheets and outcome-independent process grading.
