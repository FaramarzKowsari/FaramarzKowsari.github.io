About this book

Confluence Trading treats confluence as a decision framework rather than a score built by stacking indicators. The central question is whether genuinely different evidence families agree at a meaningful location under explicit trigger and risk rules. The book distinguishes independent evidence from duplicate evidence, puts structure and location before indicators, and shows how conflicting evidence can weaken or cancel a trade idea.

The progression moves through market structure, zones, price action, support and resistance, Fibonacci, moving averages, momentum, volume and multiple timeframes before turning those observations into a trade thesis, entry, invalidation, position size and setup-specific playbook. Trend pullbacks, breakouts and retests, reversals and range-bound conditions are used to show how confluence works when evidence agrees—and when the correct output is no trade.

What you will learn

  • Separate independent evidence from duplicate evidence that answers the same analytical question.
  • Build the market map before looking for confirmation signals.
  • Use zones and location as filters rather than demanding perfect lines.
  • Combine price action, structure, momentum, volume and trend tools without confluence shopping.
  • Assign different jobs to different timeframes and handle disagreement explicitly.
  • Turn analysis into a thesis with a trigger, invalidation condition and risk geometry.
  • Create a personal confluence playbook and grade the quality of the process separately from the outcome.

Who this book is for

For intermediate traders who already use several technical tools but want a coherent method for deciding which evidence matters, which evidence is redundant and when conflicting information should prevent a trade.
