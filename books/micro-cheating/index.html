About this book

Micro Cheating examines small, repeated behaviors that can erode trust before a relationship reaches the territory of a conventional affair. It distinguishes privacy from secrecy, friendship from emotional outsourcing, ordinary human error from recurring concealment, and forgiveness from self-abandonment. Across phones, messages, likes, former partners, workplace closeness, emotional backup relationships, boundaries, confession, repair, staying, leaving, and mature fidelity, the book seeks clarity rather than surveillance.

What you will learn

  • Distinguish privacy from secrecy and friendship from emotional displacement.
  • Assess small behaviors through repetition, concealment, intention, and impact rather than labels alone.
  • Talk about digital boundaries before mistrust becomes surveillance.
  • Recognize the difference between a repairable mistake and a recurring pattern.
  • Protect self-respect while deciding whether trust can be rebuilt or a relationship should end.
