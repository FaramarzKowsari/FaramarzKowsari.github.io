Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

ChatGPT 6 Astra is a September 2026 launch-edition guide focused on the practical shift from asking an AI for advice to delegating reviewable end-to-end work. The book examines computer use, browser-based workflows, finished documents, spreadsheets and presentations, websites and lightweight apps, coding and testing, 3D and CAD work, games, long-context research, real-world web tasks, and multi-step delegation. It emphasizes outcome-based instructions, explicit boundaries, definitions of done, mid-course steering, approval gates, testing, and human judgment rather than treating benchmark scores or model confidence as proof. Later chapters compare Astra with GPT-5.6, Gemini and Grok, discuss when a frontier model is worth the extra cost, and provide a delegation card, developer quickstart, twenty before-and-after request patterns, seven launch labs, a buyer’s guide and time-bounded verified source notes.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Understand the practical difference between a stronger answer model and a system designed to carry multi-step work toward a finished artifact.
  • Delegate outcomes with goals, context, boundaries, freedom, a definition of done and explicit stop conditions.
  • Test computer use, artifact creation, steering and restraint with observable tasks rather than trivia or prose-only demos.
  • Use AI for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, websites, code and visual or spatial work while keeping verification in the workflow.
  • Treat long context as working capacity rather than perfect memory and structure evidence accordingly.
  • Choose a cheaper model when it already meets the acceptance standard and reserve Astra for tasks where integrated capabilities reduce repair or iteration.
  • Design approval gates before granting consequential or irreversible permissions.
  • Interpret launch benchmarks and vendor-reported evaluations with attention to harnesses, task design, price and the limits of generalization.
