Description publique originale du livre

anglais

Let Them Leave, But Do Not Leave Yourself is a literary and practical guide to what remains after a person, role, job, friendship, home, dream, or old identity leaves. Its concern is not condemning departure but preventing the person left behind from disappearing from their own body, work, sleep, self-respect, calendar, and future. Across twenty-one chapters, the book moves through ambiguous endings, the mind’s demand for explanations, holding on, silent begging, the empty place, returning to the body, leaving waiting mode, making room for emptiness, refusing to build an identity around the wound, allowing joy back in, and letting the absent become ordinary again. The central discipline is simple: let departure be real without granting it final authority over the life that remains.

Objectifs d'apprentissage publiés

anglais

  • Recognize endings that arrive through changed behavior rather than a formal goodbye.
  • Separate another person’s decision to leave from the value of the life that remains.
  • Stop turning unanswered questions into an endless internal courtroom.
  • Return attention to body, sleep, work, money, friendships and ordinary routines before rewriting the whole future.
  • Allow empty space and grief without letting either become the government of daily life.
  • Rebuild through small acts of self-loyalty rather than hostility toward whoever left.
