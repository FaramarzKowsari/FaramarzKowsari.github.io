About this book

Mastering Renko Trading explains what changes when a chart is built from price movement rather than the passage of time. It begins with brick construction and brick-size choices—fixed, ATR and percentage—then develops a practical language for trend, pullback, range, continuation, two-brick reversal, support and resistance, breakout and retest.

A major theme is the difference between a cleaner chart and a more certain market. The book repeatedly warns about synthetic prices, projection or recalculating bricks and backtests that appear to fill orders at prices that were never executable. Entry, stop, exit and position-size rules are therefore tied back to real market prices and explicit invalidation. Historical and synthetic visuals are used to make the construction and limitations inspectable rather than mystical.

What you will learn

  • Understand how Renko bricks form and what information the transformation removes.
  • Choose and document a brick-size method instead of changing it after seeing the chart.
  • Read trend, pullback, range, continuation and reversal states on Renko charts.
  • Define breakout and retest rules, entry envelopes, stops and exits.
  • Use moving averages and RSI as contextual filters rather than automatic signals.
  • Separate synthetic Renko levels from executable market prices.
  • Test Renko strategies while accounting for recalculation, platform differences and realistic execution.

Who this book is for

For traders who want to use Renko as a noise-filtering analytical surface without confusing visual simplicity with certainty. Suitable for readers moving from basic chart familiarity toward rule-based testing and risk control.
