About this book

Breakout & Retest Trading begins before the breakout candle appears. The trader first defines context, a meaningful level, what qualifies as a break, what acceptance should look like, and what kind of retest and trigger would justify participation. The operating sequence is Context → Level → Break → Acceptance → Retest → Trigger → Invalidation → Target Space → Risk → Execution.

The book separates a genuine change in auction behavior from a simple penetration of a line. It examines close and acceptance, follow-through, volume, overextension, role reversal, first versus repeated retests, three entry models, false breakouts, failed retests, volatility shocks, setup grading, position sizing, backtesting, forward testing and journal design. A mandatory condition that fails leads to no trade rather than a rewritten story.

What you will learn

  • Choose levels worth breaking before the market creates urgency.
  • Define a breakout using context, acceptance and follow-through rather than wick penetration alone.
  • Recognize role reversal and distinguish a useful retest from a weak or failed return to the level.
  • Compare first, shallow, deep and repeated retests under stable rules.
  • Use pre-defined entry triggers instead of improvising after a large breakout candle.
  • Locate invalidation and target space before calculating risk and position size.
  • Identify bull traps, bear traps and failed retests without outcome-based relabeling.
  • Backtest and forward-test a frozen rules engine with realistic costs and no-trade cases.
