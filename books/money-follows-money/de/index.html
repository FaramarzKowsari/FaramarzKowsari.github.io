Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

Money Follows Money is a practical business-building framework centered on economic value rather than wealth promises. The title does not mean that money magically attracts money; it means that businesses move closer to purchase decisions when they solve problems tied to revenue, cost, time, risk, error, or productive capacity. The book teaches readers to identify expensive recurring problems, understand the buyer before building, turn complaints into market clues, sell reduced friction, test manually before automating, validate a sale before overbuilding, measure what happens after purchase, and convert customer success into product knowledge. Later chapters develop entry, core, and professional products, useful free offers, evidence-based expertise, searchable content, systems, pricing, trust, productization, ecosystems, reinvestment, and a final value-machine loop linking problem, tool, customer action, result, proof, trust, distribution, learning, and a better tool.

For creators, consultants, founders, freelancers, educators and small-business builders who want a grounded method for turning real customer problems into useful products, services and systems.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Identify business problems close to revenue, cost, time, risk or productive capacity without making wealth promises.
  • Study the buyer, existing spending and recurring complaints before building a product.
  • Start with a usable tool or service and validate transactions before automating or expanding.
  • Measure the outcome after purchase and turn customer success or failure into product knowledge.
  • Build product ladders, pricing and content around demonstrated value rather than status claims.
  • Turn repeatable delivery into systems and connected offerings without trapping customers in unnecessary complexity.
  • Treat trust as an economic asset and reinvest part of improved economics into a stronger value loop.
