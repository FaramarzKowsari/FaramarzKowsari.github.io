About this book

Turtle Soup Strategy focuses on the information that arrives after a breakout fails. The original framework begins with a prior 20-period high or low, a fresh break beyond that reference and a defined reclaim or failure condition. The book keeps the historical Turtle Soup and Turtle Soup Plus One rules separate from later language such as liquidity sweep, market structure and Fair Value Gap so that each version remains testable rather than being rewritten after the result.

The practical progression covers bullish failed breakdowns, bearish failed breakouts, Plus One timing, context, entry models, stops, invalidation, profit taking, timeframes, sessions, execution quality and confluence. Failed setups and dangerous look-alikes receive their own treatment. Eight case studies lead into backtesting and a personal playbook that fixes the market universe, reference-age rule, trigger, invalidation, position size, trade management and cancellation conditions before an order is sent.

What you will learn

  • Identify the prior 20-period reference and preserve the original timing and age rules.
  • Distinguish a genuine failed breakout and reclaim from simply fading every new high or low.
  • Separate the original Turtle Soup rule from the Turtle Soup Plus One variation and from modern adaptations.
  • Use liquidity-sweep language as optional interpretation without allowing it to rewrite the historical setup.
  • Define entry, invalidation, stop distance, profit management and position size before the trade is live.
  • Recognize failed Turtle Soups, look-alikes and setups whose required risk makes them untradeable.
  • Backtest versioned rules and build a personal playbook that reduces decisions made in the emotional moment.
