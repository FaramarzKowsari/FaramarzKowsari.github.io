A Practical Visual Trading Guide to Finding Imbalances, Reading Price Reactions, Timing Entries, and Managing Risk

Faramarz Kowsari · English · Trading · 2026

About this book

Fair Value Gaps begins with a strict observable definition: a bullish FVG exists when Candle 3 low is above Candle 1 high, while a bearish FVG exists when Candle 3 high is below Candle 1 low. From there the book separates geometry from mythology. A valid rectangle does not prove why the move occurred, does not guarantee a revisit and does not become a trade plan until context, confirmation, invalidation, costs and risk are defined.

The book follows the full lifecycle of an FVG—fresh, touched, mitigated, traversed or invalidated—and examines gap size, stacking, displacement, market structure, liquidity sweeps, higher-timeframe location, premium/discount, inverse FVGs and the midpoint or consequent-encroachment concept. Four entry models, stop and target logic, failed trades, no-trade examples, position sizing, backtesting and journal fields make the framework measurable rather than purely visual.

What you will learn

  • Mark bullish and bearish FVGs with a strict wick-to-wick three-candle definition.
  • Separate an observable imbalance from stories about why it formed.
  • Track a gap through fresh, touched, mitigated, traversed and invalidated states.
  • Evaluate displacement, structure, liquidity and higher-timeframe location before treating a gap as tradable.
  • Use direct-limit, confirmation, sweep-displacement-revisit and HTF-to-LTF entry models.
  • Define invalidation, stop, target and transaction-cost assumptions before entry.
  • Backtest FVG rules without look-ahead bias or changing definitions after seeing results.
