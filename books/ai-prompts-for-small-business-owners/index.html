About this book

AI Prompts for Small Business Owners is built around a simple principle: AI should improve decisions, not merely produce more text. The book starts with business context, facts versus assumptions, information gaps, safe-use boundaries and human review, then connects prompts to business model, value proposition, priorities, customer evidence, offers and pricing.

The library expands into marketing and local visibility, sales pipelines, proposals and follow-up, customer experience, reputation, SOPs, quality control, delegation, finance and growth. Prompts are structured around inputs, guardrails, process, outputs, measures and review conditions so they can become repeatable operating tools. The book also warns against feeding sensitive customer, employee, payment, contract or credential data into AI systems and treats consequential decisions as requiring appropriate human and professional verification.

What you will learn

  • Turn a vague business problem into a clear AI-assisted decision question.
  • Separate facts, assumptions, estimates and missing information before asking AI for recommendations.
  • Use prompts to clarify business model, value proposition, bottlenecks and 90-day priorities.
  • Extract useful customer evidence and translate it into offer, marketing and service decisions.
  • Build repeatable marketing, sales and follow-up workflows with measurable next steps.
  • Draft SOPs, role maps, quality checks and delegation tools without removing necessary human judgment.
  • Use AI with explicit guardrails for sensitive information and consequential decisions.
  • Attach measures, review dates and stop-or-revise conditions so AI-assisted work remains auditable.
