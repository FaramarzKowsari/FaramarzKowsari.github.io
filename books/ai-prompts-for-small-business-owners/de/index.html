Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

AI Prompts for Small Business Owners is designed to make generative AI useful inside the constraints of a real small business. Instead of asking what AI can do in the abstract, the book starts with the decision or work product that needs to improve, then fills prompts with concrete business context, evidence, constraints, risk and a named human decision owner. It repeatedly separates facts, assumptions and estimates and treats missing information as something to expose rather than silently invent. The prompt library spans connecting AI to the business, business models and priorities, customer and demand evidence, marketing and local visibility, sales, operations, customer experience, finance and growth. Reusable templates include auditability, version ownership, sensitive-data removal, human review and measurable next steps. Legal, tax, accounting, employment, privacy and other consequential decisions are explicitly kept under professional and human responsibility rather than delegated to the model.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Turn vague business requests into evidence-based decision questions with clear owners and constraints.
  • Separate facts, assumptions, estimates and missing information before asking AI for recommendations.
  • Use prompts across strategy, customers, marketing, sales, operations, finance and growth without losing business context.
  • Build reusable prompt templates with versioning, review criteria and explicit human checkpoints.
  • Protect sensitive data and keep consequential legal, financial, employment and customer decisions under appropriate human review.
