About this book

False Breakouts / Fakeouts is built around one practical distinction: price trading beyond a line is not the same thing as price being accepted beyond it. The book defines penetration, reclaim, acceptance and invalidation so failed breakouts can be studied as observable sequences rather than recognized only after reversal is obvious.

The framework covers support and resistance, range boundaries, previous-day and previous-week levels, trendlines, wick versus close behavior, displacement, volume, retests, liquidity sweeps, stop runs, structure, Fair Value Gaps, multiple timeframes, sessions and news. It then turns those observations into sweep-and-reclaim, failed-retest and failed-auction setups, a decision tree, entry models, risk rules, backtesting, journaling and a personal playbook.

What you will learn

  • Separate penetration beyond a level from sustained acceptance beyond it.
  • Use reclaim behavior, follow-through and structure to classify potential fakeouts.
  • Recognize false breakouts at support, resistance, ranges, prior highs and lows, and trend references.
  • Interpret liquidity sweeps and stop runs without assuming every penetration must reverse.
  • Use displacement, volume, retest behavior and multi-timeframe context as supporting evidence.
  • Apply sweep-and-reclaim, failed-breakout retest and failed-auction models with defined triggers.
  • Place invalidation and targets from the setup rather than from hindsight.
  • Build a journal and backtest that preserve losing fakeouts and explicit no-trade decisions.
