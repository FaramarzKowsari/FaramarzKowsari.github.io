About this book

CHoCH & BOS: Reading Market Structure Shifts treats structure as a language for describing continuation, damage and transition rather than as an automatic signal. It begins with swing selection, higher highs and higher lows, lower highs and lower lows, protected levels, internal versus external structure and the discipline of using stable pivot rules.

Under the book’s operating dictionary, BOS is a continuation-side close beyond a meaningful swing in an already established structural state, while CHoCH is the first meaningful opposite-side violation of the protected swing. MSS is reserved for a stronger transition with additional evidence such as displacement and subsequent swing development. The later chapters address acceptance, reclaim, timeframe hierarchy, invalidation, case studies, algorithmic state logging and journaling without renaming failed events after the outcome.

What you will learn

  • Select meaningful swings with declared rules instead of relabeling structure after the outcome.
  • Separate internal structure from the slower external structure that controls the chosen trading horizon.
  • Use protected highs and lows to define what preserves the prevailing state.
  • Apply a stable definition of BOS as continuation and CHoCH as the first meaningful opposite-side structural violation.
  • Understand why CHoCH can lead to reversal, range or failure rather than guaranteeing a new trend.
  • Use displacement, acceptance and reclaim as additional information without rewriting the original event label.
  • Keep anchor and execution timeframes fixed so invalidation cannot be rescued by timeframe hopping.
  • Record structural state and outcomes separately for honest backtesting and algorithmic analysis.
