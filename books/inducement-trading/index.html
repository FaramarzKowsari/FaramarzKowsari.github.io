About this book

Inducement Trading treats IDM as a testable liquidity hypothesis rather than a hindsight label. A visible swing, equal high or low, trendline touch, small order block or shallow imbalance may attract orders before price reaches a deeper point of interest. The book asks the trader to identify that possibility in advance and to distinguish what can be observed on a candle chart from stories about hidden institutional intent.

The framework is built in sequence: structural context, dealing range, candidate inducement, deeper POI, sweep, reaction, confirmation and invalidation. Order blocks, Fair Value Gaps, premium/discount, equal highs and lows, displacement and multi-timeframe hierarchy are incorporated without letting vocabulary replace evidence. Bullish and bearish case studies, failed setups, model construction, backtesting, journaling and a final playbook make the concept accountable to outcomes rather than protected by retrospective explanations.

What you will learn

  • Define inducement operationally as a visible liquidity-bearing reference that may be crossed before a deeper POI.
  • Rank visible levels by structural consequence instead of labeling every minor swing as IDM.
  • Preselect the deeper point of interest before the sweep occurs.
  • Separate a liquidity event from confirmation and require evidence after price reaches the relevant location.
  • Use dealing range, premium/discount, order blocks, FVGs and displacement to improve context.
  • Distinguish minor internal inducement from levels whose failure would invalidate the larger thesis.
  • Recognize failed inducement sequences and stop the model when the protected structure is broken.
  • Test the concept prospectively with replay, journaling and unchanged definitions.
