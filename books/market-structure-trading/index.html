About this book

Market Structure Trading organizes price into meaningful swing hierarchy instead of labeling every pivot equally. It develops continuation through BOS, early warning through CHoCH and a stricter MSS standard for cases where the old structural map becomes a worse explanation of price. Internal and external structure, protected highs and lows, trend, range and transition states give each label a decision role.

Liquidity sweeps, displacement, momentum, Fair Value Gaps, dealing ranges, equilibrium, premium/discount and top-down analysis are added as contextual layers. The book repeatedly warns that a sweep is not proof of reversal and that terminology differs across practitioner communities; what matters is stating the convention, defining invalidation and building one coherent map whose assumptions can be abandoned when the evidence changes.

Inside the complete Market Structure Trading guide

Market Structure Trading develops a defined progression from Price Is Not Random Noise through Liquidity Through the Lens of Structure to Complete Market Structure Case Studies. Rather than treating each term as a ready-made signal, the original contents invite readers to connect the market observation, its surrounding context and the conditions under which it would stop being useful.
