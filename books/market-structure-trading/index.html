About this book

Market Structure Trading builds a conditional market map from swing relationships rather than treating BOS, CHoCH and MSS as interchangeable labels. It starts with HH, HL, LH and LL, then distinguishes structural swings from minor pivots, trend from range and transition, and external structure from the internal path that unfolds inside it.

The book declares its terminology explicitly: BOS is a continuation break through a meaningful reference in the prevailing context; CHoCH is an early structural warning; MSS is reserved for a more consequential replacement of the operative map. Liquidity, displacement, dealing-range location, premium/discount and multi-timeframe alignment are then connected to execution and risk. Raw and annotated historical redraws, case studies and workbook exercises require the reader to rebuild the map without hindsight.

What you will learn

  • Build a market map from comparable swings and meaningful structural references.
  • Distinguish pivots from structural swings and internal structure from external structure.
  • Classify trend, range and transition without forcing every chart into a directional label.
  • Use clearly stated definitions for BOS, CHoCH and MSS.
  • Integrate liquidity, displacement, dealing-range location and premium/discount into structural analysis.
  • Assign context and tactical jobs to different timeframes.
  • Translate the map into entry, invalidation, target and risk conditions—and recognize when no trade is the structural output.
