Description publique originale du livre

anglais

Every Thought Is a Prayer uses prayer as a metaphor for repeated inner direction: what attention, language, habits, fear, stories, and daily choices repeatedly rehearse. Across thirteen chapters it moves from attention and dreams through self-talk, fear, habits, personal stories, realignment, inner consent, morning direction, midday correction, evening review, and the patience required when change is slow. The book explicitly avoids the claim that tragedy, illness, injustice, or loss are self-created; its narrower claim is that repeated inner agreements influence how a person meets reality and what kind of person is formed inside it. Each chapter returns to seeing the pattern, understanding direction, daily practice, and reflection. A thirty-day appendix translates the framework into small experiments involving silence, future-self writing, phone-free mornings, boundaries, difficult conversations, intentional work, inherited ambitions, and daily review.

Objectifs d'apprentissage publiés

anglais

  • Treat repeated attention, language, habits, and choices as directions that gradually shape identity and behavior.
  • Notice when a stated desire and a practiced inner direction move in opposite ways.
  • Recognize fear, resentment, distraction, and old stories as patterns that can be rehearsed without conscious choice.
  • Use morning orientation, midday correction, and evening review to interrupt inherited momentum.
  • Realign through small credible actions rather than dramatic declarations or perfection.
  • Hold personal responsibility without pretending that illness, injustice, tragedy, or loss are self-created.
  • Use reflection to identify old agreements, hidden loyalties, and values the calendar does not support.
  • Translate insight into a repeatable thirty-day practice of attention, boundaries, honest language, and intentional action.
