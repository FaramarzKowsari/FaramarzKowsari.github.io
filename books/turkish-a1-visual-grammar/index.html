About this combined A1 edition

Turkish A1 Visual Grammar is a highly visual beginner course that turns Turkish grammar into short, illustrated learning missions. The published edition combines the content of the original Books 1-5 and the A1 Booster into a single 307-page A1 collection, with a strong emphasis on grammar, vocabulary, sentence building and everyday Turkish.

The source material reviewed so far covers the first 150 pages of this combined product, corresponding to the material originally organized as Books 1-3. Across these pages, the course moves from survival-level words and the Turkish sound system into nominal sentences, possession and existence, then into verbs, the present continuous, direction cases, time expressions and daily routines.

Original Book 1 material — A1 foundations

  • Greetings such as Merhaba, Günaydın, İyi akşamlar and Görüşürüz.
  • The Turkish alphabet, including special letters such as Ç, Ğ, I, İ, Ö and Ü.
  • Vowel harmony and the distinction between back/front vowel groups.
  • Personal pronouns: ben, sen, o, biz, siz, onlar.
  • Plural formation with -lar / -ler.
  • Demonstratives bu, şu, o and distance-based use.
  • Question particles mı / mi / mu / mü.
  • Numbers 1-10 and basic beginner review activities.

Original Book 2 material — Nominal sentences, possession and existence

  • Nominal sentences such as Ben öğrenciyim, Sen öğretmensin and Biz arkadaşız.
  • Personal endings and vowel harmony in identity sentences.
  • Negation with değil and questions such as Sen öğrenci misin?
  • Simple demonstrative sentences: Bu ev, Şu okul, Bu ne?
  • Adjective + noun order, including examples such as büyük ev and kırmızı araba.
  • Possessive endings for my, your, his/her, our, your and their.
  • Everyday possession and family language such as kitabım, kedin, evi, okulumuz.
  • Var / yok for existence and possession, including classroom, room, family and café contexts.
  • Short visual mini-stories that combine identity, possession and existence in context.

Original Book 3 material — Actions, movement and time

  • Turkish infinitives with -mak / -mek and common verbs such as okumak, yazmak, yemek, içmek.
  • Present continuous with -yor, including person forms such as okuyorum, yazıyorsun, geliyorlar.
  • Negative present continuous patterns such as uyumuyorum and çalışmıyoruz.
  • Present-continuous questions with -yor mu? and question words such as ne, nereye, kim.
  • Directional case system: locative -da/-de/-ta/-te, dative -a/-e/-ya/-ye, and ablative -dan/-den/-tan/-ten.
  • Hard-consonant harmony and consonant softening in case formation.
  • Movement and location sentences such as Eve gidiyorum, Evde oturuyorum and Evden çıkıyorum.
  • Telling time, half-hours, days of the week, dün / bugün / yarın, and time-of-day vocabulary.
  • Daily-routine sequences including waking up, breakfast, going to work, working, resting and sleeping.

What learners practice

  • Recognizing Turkish suffix patterns through repeated visual examples.
  • Building complete beginner sentences instead of memorizing isolated vocabulary.
  • Using vowel harmony to choose plural, personal, possessive and case endings.
  • Asking and answering simple yes/no and information questions.
  • Talking about identity, possessions, family, objects, places, movement, actions and routines.
  • Reading short dialogues and mini-stories with English support.
  • Reviewing grammar through checklists, challenges, XP-style progress markers and visual recaps.

Who this book is for

For complete beginners, self-learners and A1 students who prefer visual explanations, repeated pattern recognition and practical sentence building. It is especially suited to learners who find conventional grammar-heavy textbooks too abstract and want to see each Turkish form broken into roots, suffixes and real examples.
