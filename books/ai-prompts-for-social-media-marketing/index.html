About this book

AI Prompts for Social Media Marketing is organized as a working prompt system rather than a bag of one-line commands. It begins by connecting generative AI to marketing goals, useful inputs, constraints and measurable outcomes. The early chapters establish strategy, channel roles, audience insight, personas, positioning, brand voice and messaging before moving into content pillars, editorial calendars, hooks, captions and social storytelling.

The prompts are designed to be copied, customized and reviewed by a human. They emphasize durable, platform-independent methods instead of claiming access to hidden algorithms or guaranteed reach. The broader library supports campaign planning, community and engagement work, conversion-oriented content, analysis, iteration and repeatable team workflows so social media production remains connected to brand fit, evidence and business objectives.

What you will learn

  • Turn vague social-media requests into prompts with clear goals, inputs, constraints and output formats.
  • Build a channel strategy that assigns distinct jobs to different platforms.
  • Create audience segments and personas from evidence rather than stereotypes.
  • Translate positioning and brand voice into practical writing rules for AI-assisted content.
  • Develop content pillars and sustainable editorial calendars tied to business objectives.
  • Generate and refine hooks, captions and social stories without relying on empty clickbait.
  • Use AI to support campaign, engagement and conversion workflows while keeping human review in the loop.
  • Evaluate AI output for accuracy, brand fit, legal considerations and changing platform conditions.
