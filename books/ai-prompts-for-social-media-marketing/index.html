About this book

AI Prompts for Social Media Marketing is built as a decision system rather than a list of writing tricks. Its 297 reusable prompts connect business goals, audience evidence, channel roles, resources, brand constraints and measurement before asking AI to create content. The book avoids claims about hidden platform algorithms and guaranteed reach, followers, leads or sales, and it requires human review for accuracy, brand fit, legal and rights considerations and changing platform conditions.

The library moves from AI foundations and strategy into audience insight, personas, content, campaigns, engagement, community, conversion, testing, critique, reporting and workflow design. Prompts make assumptions visible, use evidence from customer questions, comments, interviews and performance data, and encourage explicit tradeoffs when resources are limited. The final master prompts help turn separate tasks into a maintainable social-media operating system.

What you will learn

  • Translate business goals into clear social-media roles, priorities and measurable decisions.
  • Build audience segments from evidence without turning fictional personas or stereotypes into research.
  • Use AI for content, campaigns, engagement, community and conversion while preserving brand and legal review.
  • Design tests, critiques and reports that expose assumptions instead of hiding uncertainty behind polished output.
  • Create reusable workflows and master prompts that support a consistent social-media operating system.
