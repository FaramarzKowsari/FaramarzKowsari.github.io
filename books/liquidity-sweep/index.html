About this book

Liquidity Sweep builds a testable framework around a familiar price event: the market trades beyond an obvious high or low and then either rejects that excursion or accepts the new area. Rather than treating every stop run as proof of hidden institutional intent, the book defines liquidity references as observable chart locations and keeps claims about unseen order flow as hypotheses.

The reader moves from buy-side and sell-side references, prior-day and prior-week levels and session mapping into sweep anatomy, reclaim versus acceptance, displacement, structure, entries, invalidation, targets and filters. Failure cases, market differences, backtesting and journaling are part of the method. The book’s recurring standard is that a level must be marked before the move, the event defined before the outcome and losing samples retained.

What you will learn

  • Map visible liquidity references before price reaches them.
  • Distinguish a sweep, rejection, reclaim and genuine acceptance beyond a level.
  • Use session and timeframe context without claiming knowledge of hidden order inventory.
  • Combine liquidity events with structure, displacement and confirmation.
  • Define entry, invalidation, stop and target rules before the outcome is known.
  • Recognize failed sweeps and conditions where no trade is justified.
  • Build a repeatable testing and journaling process that includes losing samples and execution costs.
