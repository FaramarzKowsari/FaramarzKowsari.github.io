Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

You Are Too Valuable for Less defines self-worth through standards and choices rather than superiority. It asks where a person privately discounts time, health, attention, relationships, beliefs, appetite, and energy, then develops a framework for raising standards without turning life into perfectionism. Love and desire are examined alongside selectivity, unnecessary suffering, and intelligent leverage; AI is framed as a tool for reducing waste and accelerating thought, not as an authority over human judgment.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Define self-worth through behavior and standards rather than superiority.
  • Find where time, health, attention, and relationships are being sold too cheaply.
  • Raise standards without confusing them with perfectionism or needless pain.
  • Practice selectivity in love, desire, habits, and mental diet.
  • Use AI as leverage for clearer work and less waste while keeping human judgment in charge.
