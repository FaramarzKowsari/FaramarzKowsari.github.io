Description publique originale du livre

anglais

The Mathematical Anatomy of Moving Averages treats moving averages as filters that estimate a slower market state rather than as predictive lines. Its visual explanations connect observed price, latent trend, noise, memory length, weighting schemes, responsiveness, smoothness, and lag. SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, and adaptive ideas are compared through the signal-noise trade-off, convolution and weighting kernels, while causal versus centered averages expose look-ahead bias, repainting, and the difference between a beautiful historical line and a calculation that was actually available in real time.

Objectifs d'apprentissage publiés

anglais

  • Interpret moving averages as state estimates with explicit memory rather than prediction lines.
  • Compare filter length and weighting schemes through the trade-off between responsiveness and noise.
  • Understand convolution as the mathematical engine behind weighted moving averages.
  • Distinguish causal, live-usable averages from centered or future-dependent calculations.
  • Audit indicators for data provenance, real-time calculability, repainting, and informational delay.
