Descrição pública original do livro

inglês

Day Trading from Zero builds a beginner’s roadmap around chart reading, market structure, risk, testing, and disciplined use of AI. It distinguishes day trading from investing, introduces markets, instruments, spreads, slippage, fees, sessions, watchlists, candlesticks, timeframes, support and resistance, volume, trend, range, liquidity, and Fair Value Gaps, then connects them to position sizing, expectancy, leverage, margin, and liquidation. AI is used for learning, chart review, journaling, and structured thinking rather than as an oracle or permission to trade.

Objetivos de aprendizagem publicados

inglês

  • Understand the practical differences between day trading and longer-term investing.
  • Read basic chart structure, candles, timeframes, levels, volume, trends, ranges, and liquidity.
  • Calculate risk, position size, reward-to-risk, expectancy, and leverage exposure.
  • Use simulation, testing, and journaling before relying on live capital.
  • Use AI to organize learning and review evidence without treating it as a trading oracle.
