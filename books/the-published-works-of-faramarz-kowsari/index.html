About this book

The Published Works of Faramarz Kowsari is an authorship portfolio designed to make a Persian-language publishing record accessible to international professional readers. It consolidates ISBN-registered publications and records associated with the National Library and Archives of Iran, explains an editorial transparency method for reconstructed English titles and synopses, documents de-duplication by ISBN and record identifiers, preserves author order, and provides verification links where available. The selected edition organizes archival works across technology and AI, business, education, psychology and mindfulness, personal development, relationships, and creative literature.

What you will learn

  • Understand the scope and verification method used to assemble the authorship portfolio.
  • Read reconstructed English synopses as editorial descriptions rather than verbatim publisher abstracts.
  • Use ISBN and National Library record identifiers to distinguish and verify archival entries.
  • See how duplicate records and author order are handled in the portfolio.
  • Navigate a publishing history spanning technology, business, education, psychology, relationships, and literature.
