Descrição pública original do livro

inglês

AI Prompts for E-Commerce Sellers treats generative AI as a decision-support layer for a store rather than merely a copywriter. Its 278 prompts connect verified evidence, constraints, structured outputs and human review to store goals, customer insight, product research, catalog positioning, listings, search intent, product pages, conversion, offers, advertising, lifecycle messaging, support, social proof, cart recovery, retention, merchandising, localization, analytics and reusable master workflows.
