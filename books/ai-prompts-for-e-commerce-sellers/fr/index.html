Description publique originale du livre

anglais

AI Prompts for E-Commerce Sellers treats generative AI as a decision-support layer for a store rather than merely a copywriter. Its 278 prompts connect verified evidence, constraints, structured outputs and human review to store goals, customer insight, product research, catalog positioning, listings, search intent, product pages, conversion, offers, advertising, lifecycle messaging, support, social proof, cart recovery, retention, merchandising, localization, analytics and reusable master workflows.

Objectifs d'apprentissage publiés

anglais

  • Turn store goals, constraints and available data into reusable AI briefs.
  • Separate facts, assumptions, estimates and open questions before AI output is used.
  • Analyze customers, demand and product opportunities without fabricating evidence.
  • Build listings, product pages, search-aligned content and conversion briefs from verified facts.
  • Create structured workflows for ads, lifecycle messaging, support, retention and catalog operations.
  • Design localization, analytics and A/B testing workflows with explicit human review points.
  • Maintain reusable prompts with owners, variables, versioning and review triggers.
