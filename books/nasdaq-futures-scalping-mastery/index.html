Nasdaq-100 Futures · Scalping & Day Trading

The setup means nothing if the market cannot fill it well.

NASDAQ FUTURES SCALPING MASTERY by Faramarz Kowsari is written for traders who want to understand the Nasdaq-100 futures auction rather than impose familiar candlestick templates on every session. It treats NQ and MNQ as real exchange-traded contracts with particular tick values, liquidity conditions, order mechanics and risk consequences.

The book begins with contract differences, the Nasdaq trading clock, overnight behaviour, prior-day levels and the New York cash-market opening. It then turns to the tools that can clarify or challenge a chart hypothesis: Depth of Market, footprint charts, Cumulative Volume Delta, VWAP, Volume Profile, liquidity sweeps and auction failure.

Ten conditional playbooks cover opening drives, opening-range breaks and failures, overnight sweeps, session-level acceptance, VWAP pullbacks, absorption and post-news continuations. The final chapters ask whether these decisions remain worthwhile after realistic costs, missed fills, losing streaks, drawdowns and market replay. Passing on a trade is treated as part of the process, not as a weakness.

A closer look

The NQ scalp decision stack

A good-looking chart is only one layer. The book puts four checks between the setup and the order.

CONTEXT

Where are we?

Overnight range, previous-session reference points, cash-open timing, volatility regime and macro event calendar.

CONFIRM

What has actually happened?

Price acceptance or rejection, order-flow participation, rotation structure, VWAP positioning and alternative interpretations.

EXECUTE

Can the trade work after costs?

NQ or MNQ selection, instrument-specific risk, slippage, spread, order type, stop distance and no-trade rules.

Ten playbooks, one standard of evidence

The book's ten setups are not ten promises. Each is a conditional hypothesis: define the environment, identify the confirming behaviour, state the invalidation, select a practicable order, and reject the trade when spread, market speed or contradictory evidence makes the economics unacceptable.

Who This Book Is For

NQ and MNQ futures traders, active day traders, market-replay students, funded-account candidates and technically curious readers who know basic futures concepts but want a Nasdaq-specific decision and execution framework.

Scope and care

Leveraged futures can produce substantial loss. Stops do not guarantee execution at the trigger price. Broker policies, margins, fees, contract details and exchange schedules change; verify them before live trading. No setup guarantees profitability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the book about NQ and MNQ rather than NAS100 CFDs?

Yes. It distinguishes exchange-traded Nasdaq-100 futures from the cash index, QQQ and CFD representations, including differences relevant to risk and execution.

Does the book contain ten scalping playbooks?

Yes. Chapters 25–34 present ten conditional trade frameworks covering opening, liquidity, VWAP, order flow and post-news behaviour.

Does it include market replay and cost analysis?

Yes. Later chapters address the real economics of a scalp, drawdowns, historical case studies, replay robustness and a 30-session practice.

Will an opening-range breakout always work?

No. The book emphasizes acceptance versus failure, contradictory evidence, fill conditions and explicit no-trade decisions.
