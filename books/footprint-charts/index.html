Reading Buyers and Sellers Inside the Candle

Faramarz Kowsari · English · Trading

About this book

Footprint Charts expands the information hidden inside a candlestick into executed volume at price, then places that detail inside a disciplined Context → Location → Order Flow → Trigger → Risk hierarchy. It covers data-source limitations, bid × ask, delta, imbalances, absorption, exhaustion, trapped traders, delta divergence, candle POC, Volume Profile, VWAP, market structure, liquidity, volatility, reversals, breakouts, retests, invalidation, slippage, replay and backtesting without treating footprint detail as certainty.

What you will learn

  • Understand what footprint charts add to OHLC candles and what they still cannot reveal.
  • Interpret aggressive execution, bid × ask, delta and imbalances without inventing trader motives.
  • Recognize absorption, exhaustion, trapped traders and effort-versus-result divergence.
  • Combine footprint evidence with Volume Profile, VWAP, market structure, liquidity and regime context.
  • Build triggers, invalidation, stops, targets and no-trade rules around order-flow evidence.
  • Account for feed, venue, platform, slippage and execution differences.
  • Use replay and backtesting to test interpretations rather than memorizing ideal screenshots.
