About this book

Footprint Charts expands the information hidden inside a candlestick into executed volume at price, then places that detail inside a disciplined Context → Location → Order Flow → Trigger → Risk hierarchy. It covers data-source limitations, bid × ask, delta, imbalances, absorption, exhaustion, trapped traders, delta divergence, candle POC, Volume Profile, VWAP, market structure, liquidity, volatility, reversals, breakouts, retests, invalidation, slippage, replay and backtesting without treating footprint detail as certainty.

Look inside Footprint Charts before deciding to buy

The original 25-chapter source develops a learning route from The Candle Is Only the Outer Shell through Footprint Charts and Volume Profile toward From Footprint Reader to Footprint Trader. This matters because a trading concept is useful only if the reader can explain where it fits, which evidence supports it, and how an opposing outcome would change the interpretation.

Frequently asked questions

How many topics are covered in Footprint Charts?

The source contents contain 25 consecutive numbered chapters, starting at “The Candle Is Only the Outer Shell” and reaching “From Footprint Reader to Footprint Trader.”

Is The Mechanics Behind Order Flow covered?

Yes. The manuscript includes this topic as Chapter 2.

Where can I study Footprint Charts and Volume Profile?

It is listed in Chapter 13, after the opening material and before later applications.

What does the end of the book cover?

The closing numbered chapters include “Risk, Slippage, and Execution Reality”, “Replay, Backtesting, and Deliberate Practice”, “From Footprint Reader to Footprint Trader”.

Are chart illustrations guarantees of strategy performance?

No. Chart-based methods can fail. Readers should evaluate risk, execution, transaction costs and the difference between illustrative examples and documented historical data.

What you will learn

  • Understand what footprint charts add to OHLC candles and what they still cannot reveal.
  • Interpret aggressive execution, bid × ask, delta and imbalances without inventing trader motives.
  • Recognize absorption, exhaustion, trapped traders and effort-versus-result divergence.
  • Combine footprint evidence with Volume Profile, VWAP, market structure, liquidity and regime context.
  • Build triggers, invalidation, stops, targets and no-trade rules around order-flow evidence.
  • Account for feed, venue, platform, slippage and execution differences.
  • Use replay and backtesting to test interpretations rather than memorizing ideal screenshots.
