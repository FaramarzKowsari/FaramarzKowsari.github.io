About this book

0DTE Options: Trading the Same-Day Market treats zero-days-to-expiration options as a compressed decision environment in which market, execution and behavioral risk unfold quickly. The first part builds the mechanics of same-day contracts, the option chain and the Greeks—Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega—before connecting option selection to the underlying market structure and the changing character of the trading day from the opening phase through the final hour and expiration.

The strategy chapters cover directional calls and puts, debit and credit spreads, iron condors, butterflies and broken-wing butterflies, straddles, strangles, event-day volatility, momentum, breakout, continuation and mean-reversion setups. Later sections treat execution as part of the strategy, including bid-ask spread and slippage, as well as exercise, assignment, settlement, position sizing, daily loss limits, backtesting, expectancy, drawdown, MAE/MFE, psychology and journaling. The book’s operating principle is that the shortest expiration does not simplify the trade; it removes the time that can hide weak decisions.

What you will learn

  • Understand what changes when an option is opened and expires on the same trading day.
  • Read an option chain without losing sight of liquidity, spread and the underlying market.
  • Use Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega as interacting exposures rather than isolated definitions.
  • Match directional, spread, range and event-volatility structures to a clearly stated market thesis.
  • Account for the way opening conditions, midday decay and the final hour change the decision environment.
  • Treat execution quality, bid-ask spread, slippage, exercise, assignment and settlement as part of the strategy.
  • Size risk and enforce daily loss limits in a market where exposure can change rapidly.
  • Evaluate strategies with realistic fills, costs, expectancy, drawdown, MAE/MFE and post-session review.
