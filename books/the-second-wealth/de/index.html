Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

The Second Wealth is a practical manual for experienced adults who want to turn accumulated judgment, knowledge, relationships, reputation, and pattern recognition into modern services, products, and systems supported by AI. It rejects instant-income promises and begins with runway, usable experience, the limits of generative AI, customer selection, privacy, and evidence. The book then moves through market listening, expensive-problem discovery, path selection, platform work, packaged services, assessments, digital assets, micro-tools, pricing, outreach, SOPs, automation, and ownership. Part Five uses hands-on laboratories for services, writing, local-business content, landing pages, AI opportunity assessments, digital products, content engines, and one-function tools. The final ninety-day plan moves from choosing a market and building one sample to customer conversations, paid pilots, delivery, documentation, pricing, a first SOP, and a low-risk automation. Twenty practical appendices provide readiness checks, scorecards, interview guides, offer cards, pricing sheets, outreach patterns, proposal and SOP templates, privacy checks, and a ninety-day dashboard.

Für wen ist das Buch gedacht?

Die Zielgruppe ergibt sich aus der öffentlichen Buchbeschreibung. Für deutschsprachige Leser ist besonders wichtig, dass die eigentliche Ausgabe in Englisch vorliegt.

For experienced professionals, retirees, career changers, consultants, managers, educators, and adults over 50 who want a realistic, evidence-based way to combine decades of experience with modern AI tools without relying on hype, guaranteed-income claims, or unnecessary technical complexity.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Identify the economic value hidden inside accumulated experience, judgment, relationships, and pattern recognition.
  • Build a realistic financial and time runway before choosing an AI-assisted income path.
  • Use generative AI as a checked apprentice rather than as an unquestioned authority.
  • Select one market and one expensive problem through interviews, evidence, and a path-selection matrix.
  • Turn expertise into packaged services, assessments, digital assets, content systems, or small decision tools.
  • Price and scope offers with clear boundaries, measurable outcomes, and respect for privacy and professional limits.
  • Test demand through samples, outreach, discovery calls, paid pilots, and explicit decision records.
  • Document repeatable delivery, automate only after proof, and build toward owned assets and a resilient second-income system.
