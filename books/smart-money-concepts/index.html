About this book

Smart Money Concepts organizes SMC into a testable chart-reading framework rather than a vocabulary of boxes and claims about institutional intent. It keeps observable elements—structure, liquidity references, displacement, imbalance, location, reaction and invalidation—separate from interpretations that cannot be proved from a standard candlestick chart.

The book builds its grammar cumulatively: HH/HL and LH/LL sequences, BOS, CHoCH and MSS, buy-side and sell-side liquidity, Fair Value Gaps, order-block candidates, equal highs and lows, premium/discount and structural objectives. Later chapters turn those concepts into trade planning, risk control, case studies and unlabeled exercises. The emphasis is on stating a thesis before the outcome, defining invalidation without negotiation and passing when the evidence is incomplete.

What you will learn

  • Separate observable price behavior from interpretations about hidden institutional intent.
  • Read HH/HL and LH/LL sequences and apply BOS, CHoCH and MSS consistently.
  • Map buy-side and sell-side liquidity references and understand their analytical limits.
  • Use displacement, FVGs and order-block candidates as contextual evidence rather than guarantees.
  • Work with dealing ranges, premium/discount and structural objectives.
  • Build a complete trade thesis with confirmation, invalidation, target and controlled risk.
  • Practice independent chart recognition through unlabeled exercises instead of memorizing annotations.
