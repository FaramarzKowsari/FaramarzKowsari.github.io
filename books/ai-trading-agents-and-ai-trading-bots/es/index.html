Descripción pública original del libro

inglés

AI Trading Agents & AI Trading Bots treats an AI trading system as a governed workflow rather than a model attached directly to execution. It follows the path from evidence and market state through models, policy, abstention and independent risk to order-state reconciliation, portfolio constraints, testing, production architecture, security, graded autonomy and five end-to-end builds. The final sections study failure chains, backtest overfitting, uncertainty and controlled deployment, supported by implementation templates, checklists and incident dossiers.

Objetivos de aprendizaje publicados

inglés

  • Separate model proposals from permission and economic authority.
  • Build evidence-preserving data, context and memory pipelines.
  • Combine probabilistic models with deterministic policy, abstention and independent risk controls.
  • Design execution workflows that reconcile external state after partial fills, timeouts and restarts.
  • Validate systems through chronology-preserving backtests, walk-forward testing, shadow mode and staged deployment.
  • Build production services with observability, least privilege, security boundaries and recovery.
  • Use graded, reversible autonomy and analyze failure chains before expanding authority.
  • Apply five reference architectures for research, setup detection, risk-controlled execution, multi-agent coordination and copilots.
