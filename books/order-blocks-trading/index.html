About this book

Order Blocks Trading turns the popular order-block rectangle into an operational decision framework. A zone is not treated as a trade by itself. The book requires the reader to explain why a candidate qualifies, what displacement and market structure support it, what liquidity event matters, how the zone should be refined, what would invalidate it and how execution and risk should be handled.

Bullish and bearish order blocks are developed alongside liquidity and Fair Value Gaps, higher- and lower-timeframe execution, fresh versus mitigated or consumed zones, continuation and reversal contexts, and failure into breaker-style behavior. The later sections include winning, losing and failed case studies, measurable rules, backtesting, a trading journal, qualification checklists, scoring sheets and pre- and post-trade review tools.

What you will learn

  • Define an order block with explicit qualification rules rather than drawing zones retrospectively.
  • Use market structure and displacement to evaluate whether a candidate matters.
  • Distinguish bullish and bearish blocks and track freshness, mitigation and consumption.
  • Combine liquidity and FVG context without treating institutional narratives as proven facts.
  • Choose between direct-touch and confirmation-based execution.
  • Place invalidation, stops and targets according to the setup rather than the rectangle alone.
  • Convert the concept into measurable rules, backtests, journal fields and review checklists.
