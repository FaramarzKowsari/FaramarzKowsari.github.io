About this book

Candlestick Patterns From Zero is a 100-page visual course that teaches candlestick reading from basic OHLC anatomy through pattern recognition, market context, confirmation and risk-aware execution. The book treats candle patterns as clues rather than guarantees: readers learn to interpret bodies and wicks, bullish and bearish candles, doji structures, hammers, shooting stars, engulfing patterns, harami patterns, morning and evening stars, multi-candle formations and gaps while checking trend, support and resistance, volume, volatility and higher-level context. Later lessons connect recognition to practical decision rules for entry triggers, stop-loss placement, targets, risk-reward, invalidation and confirmation.

100 visual pages that teach the candle—and the decision behind it

A candlestick is four numbers arranged into a picture. It can show an open, high, low and close; it cannot explain the whole market or guarantee the next move. This visual-first course teaches readers to examine candle anatomy, bodies and wicks, context, risk and whether a familiar pattern still deserves attention once surrounding market structure is considered.

What the original illustrated book teaches

The following highlights are drawn from identifiable visual teaching pages in the original PDF rather than invented chapter headings.

See how OHLC builds the candle

The illustrated page titled “The OHLC Anatomy” (PDF p. 5) compares bullish and bearish bodies, upper/lower wicks and the distinction between recognizing a candle and executing a trade.

An inside candle is not proof of a bullish reversal

The “Bullish Harami” visual (PDF p. 34) explains a small candle contained within a larger bearish mother candle. It stresses contraction in momentum, context and confirmation instead of treating the label as a sure entry.

Combine recognition with a risk-first process

The teaching pages place psychology, observation and discipline beside execution: context check, setup validation, risk management, action and review.

Use one visual as one deliberate practice exercise

Cover a diagram's labels, try to identify the candle geometry and context, then reveal the notes. This turns a visually rich reference into a way to practice interpretation.

What you will learn

  • Read open, high, low and close structure and interpret candle bodies and wicks.
  • Recognize common bullish, bearish and neutral candlestick formations without treating them as automatic signals.
  • Judge patterns in relation to trend, support and resistance, volume, volatility and market location.
  • Wait for confirmation and define an entry trigger instead of entering solely because a pattern appears.
  • Place stops beyond logical invalidation points and map targets from chart structure.
  • Evaluate risk-reward and know when a technically attractive pattern is still a poor trade.
  • Use a confirmation ladder and reliability checklist to turn pattern literacy into a repeatable process.
