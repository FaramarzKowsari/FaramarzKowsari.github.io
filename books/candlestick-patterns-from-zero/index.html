About this book

Candlestick Patterns From Zero is a 100-page visual course that teaches candlestick reading from basic OHLC anatomy through pattern recognition, market context, confirmation and risk-aware execution. The book treats candle patterns as clues rather than guarantees: readers learn to interpret bodies and wicks, bullish and bearish candles, doji structures, hammers, shooting stars, engulfing patterns, harami patterns, morning and evening stars, multi-candle formations and gaps while checking trend, support and resistance, volume, volatility and higher-level context. Later lessons connect recognition to practical decision rules for entry triggers, stop-loss placement, targets, risk-reward, invalidation and confirmation.

What you will learn

  • Read open, high, low and close structure and interpret candle bodies and wicks.
  • Recognize common bullish, bearish and neutral candlestick formations without treating them as automatic signals.
  • Judge patterns in relation to trend, support and resistance, volume, volatility and market location.
  • Wait for confirmation and define an entry trigger instead of entering solely because a pattern appears.
  • Place stops beyond logical invalidation points and map targets from chart structure.
  • Evaluate risk-reward and know when a technically attractive pattern is still a poor trade.
  • Use a confirmation ladder and reliability checklist to turn pattern literacy into a repeatable process.
