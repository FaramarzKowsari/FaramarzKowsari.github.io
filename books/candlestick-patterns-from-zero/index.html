About this book

Candlestick Patterns From Zero is a 100-page visual course that begins with the anatomy of a candle and builds toward context-aware trade planning. The opening lessons explain open, high, low and close, the information carried by bodies and wicks, bullish and bearish candles, and the psychology behind rejection, directional pressure and indecision.

The course then develops pattern literacy across doji structures, hammers, inverted hammers, shooting stars, hanging men, engulfing and harami patterns, morning and evening stars, multi-candle formations, gaps, reversal structures and continuation clues. A recurring principle keeps the material grounded: a pattern is evidence, not a promise. The book repeatedly asks where a pattern appears, whether trend and key levels support it, and what confirmation would make the idea stronger.

The final part connects recognition to execution. Readers work through entry triggers, stop-loss placement, chart-based targets, risk-reward mapping, invalidation rules, a confirmation ladder and a pattern-reliability board. News candles and unstable conditions are treated as special risk environments rather than as invitations to force a setup.

What you will learn

  • Read OHLC structure and understand what candle bodies and upper or lower wicks communicate.
  • Recognize major bullish, bearish and neutral candlestick formations without treating them as automatic trade signals.
  • Use trend, support and resistance, market location, volume and volatility to judge whether a pattern has useful context.
  • Differentiate recognition from confirmation and wait for an entry trigger when the setup requires one.
  • Place stops around logical invalidation rather than around emotional comfort.
  • Choose targets from chart structure, measured movement or trailing logic instead of hope.
  • Compare expected reward with risk before entering and reject patterns whose trade map is unattractive.
  • Use confirmation and reliability checklists to make candlestick analysis more repeatable.
