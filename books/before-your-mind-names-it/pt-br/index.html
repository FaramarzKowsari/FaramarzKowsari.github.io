Descrição pública original do livro

inglês

Before Your Mind Names It treats mindfulness as the ability to notice the interval between an event and the name, judgment, or story the mind gives it. Rather than demanding calm, the book works with body signals, memory, anxiety, anger, shame, grief, uncertainty, silence, messages, notifications, boredom, distraction, and the temptation to use meditation as escape. Its recurring practice is to return to the unnamed space where experience can be observed before reaction becomes identity.

Objetivos de aprendizagem publicados

inglês

  • Separate observable events from the stories and labels that rapidly follow them.
  • Notice body signals before they are absorbed into automatic interpretations.
  • Work with anxiety, anger, shame, grief, and uncertainty without forcing calm.
  • Recognize when meditation or distraction is being used to avoid experience.
  • Use short return practices to widen the space between stimulus and response.
