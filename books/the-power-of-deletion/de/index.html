Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

The Power of Deletion treats removal as an act of attention and access management rather than cruelty. It explores the hidden cost of keeping people, habits, digital noise, guilt, status games, and internal loops that repeatedly disturb the life a person is trying to build. Family, friendship, love, work, social media, body signals, standards, gradual deletion, returning people, and clean boundary language are examined alongside a seven-day practice for creating space after removal.

For readers who want clearer boundaries and less repeated disruption without turning self-protection into hostility.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Treat deletion as a decision about access and attention rather than revenge.
  • Identify the hidden tax of repeated disturbance in relationships, habits, and digital life.
  • Use cleaner boundaries in family, friendship, love, and work.
  • Distinguish guilt from a genuine obligation to keep harmful access open.
  • Build a calmer life after removal instead of leaving an empty space.
