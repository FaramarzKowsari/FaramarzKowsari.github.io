About this book

Claude AI Side Hustle is a visual catalog of 200 service and product ideas organized around content creation, AI writing, business support, design, online courses, automation, marketing, and digital products. Its recurring five-stage funnel moves from discovering an opportunity to creating, delivering, marketing, and scaling it. Each visual idea identifies what can be sold, who may buy it, how Claude can assist, a possible pricing model, a first-sale action, startup effort, cash-speed and repeatability indicators, plus a reality check against exaggerated promises.

What you will learn

  • Scan 200 concrete side-hustle formats across multiple service and product categories.
  • Define a sellable deliverable and a plausible buyer before building an offer.
  • Use Claude to accelerate research, drafting, customization, and delivery without inventing client facts.
  • Compare startup effort, cash speed, repeatability, and per-project or package models.
  • Move from opportunity discovery to delivery, marketing, referrals, and scaling.
