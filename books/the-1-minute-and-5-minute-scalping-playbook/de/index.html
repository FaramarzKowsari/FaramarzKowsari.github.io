Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

The 1-Minute & 5-Minute Scalping Playbook assigns different jobs to the two timeframes: the 5-minute chart provides context, structure, and location, while the 1-minute chart is used as an execution lens. It develops precision entries, transaction-cost awareness, stops, position sizing, targets, fast trade management, no-trade conditions, journaling, replay, and backtesting. Eight setup playbooks cover trend pullbacks, liquidity sweeps and reclaims, breakouts and retests, failed breakouts, opening-range momentum, VWAP reactions, compression-to-expansion, and 5-minute reversal with 1-minute confirmation.

For active short-term traders who want a rules-based scalping process rather than improvised one-minute entries.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Separate context and execution by assigning distinct jobs to 5-minute and 1-minute charts.
  • Use defined setup rules instead of reacting to every fast candle.
  • Account for spread, slippage, fees, stops, sizing, and daily loss limits.
  • Recognize abnormal, news-driven, and overtrading conditions where no trade is justified.
  • Test and journal setups through replay, simulation, metrics, and a 30-session practice program.
