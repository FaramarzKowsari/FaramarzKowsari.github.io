About this book

Multi-Timeframe Analysis treats different charts as a hierarchy of jobs rather than a collection of opinions. The method moves deliberately from regime and higher-timeframe context to bias, location, setup, trigger, invalidation, target and management. Broad charts provide context; narrow charts provide timing. Neither is allowed to rewrite the other after the trade is already underway.

The book develops role-based timeframe stacks for scalping, intraday trading and swing trading, explains nested structure and disagreement between charts, and emphasizes that lower-timeframe detail becomes useful only after price reaches a location where an entry trigger has meaning. Failure modes such as timeframe hopping, zoom-in noise and no-trade denial are treated explicitly, then the complete decision architecture is frozen and tested in replay.

What you will learn

  • Assign a specific job to every timeframe instead of letting every chart vote equally.
  • Build timeframe stacks from the intended holding horizon for scalping, intraday and swing trading.
  • Separate market regime, context, directional bias, location, setup and entry trigger.
  • Understand nested trends and why lower-timeframe counter-moves do not automatically invalidate higher-timeframe context.
  • Use lower timeframes only when price reaches a pre-defined location where a trigger matters.
  • Prevent timeframe hopping from moving the invalidation goalpost after a thesis weakens.
  • Define no-trade outcomes when evidence is mixed or location is poor.
  • Backtest the full hierarchy with frozen definitions and information available at the time.
