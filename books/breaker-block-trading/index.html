About this book

Breaker Block Trading starts where an order-block expectation fails. Rather than treating a breaker as a magical rectangle, the book reads it as a change of state: an earlier bullish or bearish thesis is violated, price reprices through meaningful structure, and the former zone may later be tested from the opposite side.

The framework builds from order blocks and failure anatomy into liquidity, displacement, Fair Value Gaps, dealing-range location, higher- and lower-timeframe mapping, retests, entry models, invalidation, targets and position risk. Failed setups, low-quality breakers, rule-based checklists, case studies, replay testing and a compact playbook are included so the concept can be evaluated as a repeatable decision process rather than a hindsight label.

What you will learn

  • Distinguish a true breaker sequence from an ordinary support or resistance retest.
  • Identify the order-block expectation that failed and the structural event that changed the market state.
  • Evaluate bullish and bearish breakers using liquidity, displacement, location and timeframe hierarchy.
  • Compare breaker blocks with order blocks, mitigation blocks and Fair Value Gaps without mixing their definitions.
  • Use retest quality and lower-timeframe evidence to design entries without chasing price.
  • Define invalidation, stop placement, position risk and target logic before execution.
  • Reject low-quality or over-explained breaker setups and document no-trade decisions.
  • Test the model with replay, stable rules, losing examples and a structured trading journal.
