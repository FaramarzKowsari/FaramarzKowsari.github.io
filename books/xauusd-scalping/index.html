About this book

XAUUSD Scalping treats intraday gold as its own market ecosystem rather than simply another forex ticker. It begins with how London OTC activity, US futures and Asian trading interact, how broker-specific XAUUSD products can differ, and why spreads, slippage, leverage and macro releases matter more when the expected holding period is short.

The trading framework maps the market before discussing entries. Liquidity, structure, displacement, Fair Value Gaps, VWAP, session ranges and candle behavior are used as pieces of evidence, not magic signals. Ten setup models, execution rules, event-risk protocols, complete case studies and a visual scalper checklist turn the material into a repeatable playbook. Failed trades, skipped trades and missed entries are deliberately included so the reader learns process rather than hindsight.

What you will learn

  • Understand why intraday gold must be treated as its own ecosystem with product-specific execution conditions.
  • Map Asian, London and New York session structure and liquidity before looking for entries.
  • Use structure, liquidity, displacement, FVGs, VWAP and candle behavior as complementary evidence.
  • Distinguish a liquidity sweep from genuine breakout acceptance.
  • Apply defined scalping setups with entry qualification, invalidation and trade-management rules.
  • Size positions from planned loss rather than desired profit.
  • Use a news-risk protocol and recognize when the correct trade is no trade.
  • Review complete cases and turn the setups into a tested personal XAUUSD playbook.
