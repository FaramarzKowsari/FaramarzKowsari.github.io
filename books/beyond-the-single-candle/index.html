About this book

Beyond the Single Candle is a focused field guide to reading the relationship between one candle and the next rather than treating isolated candlestick shapes as predictions. It builds from the geometry of range, body and close into Inside Bars, compression, mother-bar breaks, false breakouts, engulfing patterns and Outside Bars, always asking what changed, where it happened and what evidence would invalidate the idea.

The book then places those relationships inside market context. It compares 15-minute, 1-hour and daily views, separates a breakout event from an entry rule, and treats invalidation and risk as part of the setup rather than an afterthought. A twelve-case laboratory, observation worksheet, playbook template and backtest log turn pattern recognition into a repeatable decision process.

What you will learn

  • Read the relationship between two consecutive candles instead of relying on isolated pattern names.
  • Distinguish range, body, close location, containment, expansion and body takeover.
  • Evaluate Inside Bars, compression, engulfing patterns, Outside Bars and false breaks in context.
  • Separate a breakout event from an actual entry rule.
  • Use location and timeframe to decide whether a two-candle pattern has a meaningful job.
  • Define invalidation, stop logic and risk before capital is committed.
  • Build and test a personal two-candle playbook with repeatable observation rules.

Who this book is for

Designed for traders who know basic candlesticks and want a more disciplined way to read price action without memorizing dozens of pattern names. It is especially useful for discretionary price-action traders who want clearer context, invalidation and risk rules.
