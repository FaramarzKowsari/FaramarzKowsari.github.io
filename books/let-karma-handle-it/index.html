About this book

Let Karma Handle It reframes karma as a language of return and balance rather than a threat, superstition, or excuse for passivity. Its practical concern is what happens when a person stops spending attention on revenge, proving, surveillance, and the wish to administer consequences personally. Across themes of betrayal, anger, boundaries, silence, forgiveness, prosperity, joy, and a thirty-day practice map, the book asks the reader to protect conduct and energy without becoming an assistant to punishment.

What you will learn

  • Separate karma from threats, magical thinking, and passive avoidance.
  • Recognize the attention and energy cost of revenge and constant proof-seeking.
  • Use boundaries and distance without turning them into punishment theatre.
  • Distinguish forgiveness from renewed access or denial of harm.
  • Build a life organized around conduct, peace, joy, and forward movement.

Who this book is for

For readers trying to move beyond betrayal, resentment, or revenge without surrendering boundaries or self-respect.
