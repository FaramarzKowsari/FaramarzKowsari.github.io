Originale öffentliche Buchbeschreibung

Englisch

You Can’t Control Life, But You Can Train Your Response focuses on the agency that remains when events refuse to obey plans. It treats emotions as weather rather than commands and develops pause, attention, body awareness, language, boundaries, delayed response, flexible planning, and wise action across failure, delay, loss, work, money, relationships, and digital overstimulation. The closing framework helps the reader build a personal response protocol for difficult days and unpredictable situations.

Für wen ist das Buch gedacht?

Die Zielgruppe ergibt sich aus der öffentlichen Buchbeschreibung. Für deutschsprachige Leser ist besonders wichtig, dass die eigentliche Ausgabe in Englisch vorliegt.

For readers who want greater stability and wiser action without pretending that life itself can be fully controlled.

Ausgewiesene Lernziele

Englisch

  • Separate uncontrollable events from trainable responses.
  • Read emotions as information without treating them as automatic instructions.
  • Use pause, delayed response, attention, and body awareness to slow reaction.
  • Apply flexible plans and boundaries in work, money, relationships, and loss.
  • Create a personal response protocol for difficult and unpredictable days.
