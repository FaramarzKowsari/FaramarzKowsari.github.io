Futures Trading Books: Micro E-mini, Index Futures & Execution

Futures trading combines chart analysis with contract mechanics that directly affect risk and execution. These books cover Micro E-mini and index futures, contract specifications, tick value, margin, session structure, order flow, VWAP, volume profile, slippage, backtesting and professional execution. They are intended for traders who want to understand not only where a setup appears on the chart, but also what instrument is being traded, how the contract behaves, and how sizing and execution translate an idea into real account exposure.

FUTURES TRADING FROM ZERO

The Complete Practical Guide to Micro E-mini, E-mini & E-Nano Futures, Contract Mechanics, Margin, Risk Management, Price Action, Order Flow, Backtesting, and Professional Execution

FUTURES TRADING FROM ZERO is built around a simple premise: futures trading becomes dangerous when a trader learns patterns before learning the contract. This book therefore starts below the chart. It explains what a futures contract is, how an exchange, clearinghouse, broker and data feed divide responsibilities, how a buy order becomes an open position, and why the same market move can represent radically different dollar risk depending on the contract family being traded.

MICRO E-MINI FUTURES TRADING

A Practical Guide to MES & MNQ Futures, Market Structure, Intraday Strategies, Execution, and Risk

MICRO E-MINI FUTURES TRADING is a practical, evidence-conscious guide to trading the two Micro E-mini equity-index contracts most closely watched by active U.S. index traders: MES, which tracks the S&P 500, and MNQ, which tracks the Nasdaq-100. The book begins before strategy selection. It explains what a futures contract actually represents, how leverage and variation in price translate into dollars, why posted margin is not the same thing as maximum risk, and why a smaller contract can still create losses that are large relative to a small account.
