Gold, Forex, Silver & Crypto Trading Books

Different markets impose different execution environments. Gold and silver can react sharply to macroeconomic information and liquidity windows; forex is shaped by currency sessions and pair structure; crypto trades continuously and carries its own leverage and liquidation dynamics. This collection focuses on market-specific behavior in XAUUSD, XAGUSD and crypto-related trading, while keeping risk, liquidity, volatility and instrument mechanics at the center of the decision.

SILVER TRADING

XAGUSD Price Action, Silver Futures, Smart Money Concepts & Risk Management

SILVER TRADING is an evidence-led practical guide that connects the physical and industrial silver market with the instruments, price behavior, execution mechanics and risk decisions faced by active traders. It begins by separating silver from the simplified idea of being merely a cheaper form of gold. The book examines supply, recycling, industrial fabrication, inventories, macroeconomic forces, the gold-silver ratio and the global trading network, then asks a more important practical question: what exactly is being traded when a trader sees XAGUSD, a CFD, a futures contract, a metal-linked product or physical exposure on a screen?

XAUUSD Scalping

Trading Gold Intraday

XAUUSD Scalping begins with the mechanics and market structure that make short-horizon gold trading different: the exact product being traded, quote conventions, lot specifications, spreads, commissions, slippage and leverage. It maps the intraday clock through Asia, London, the London–New York overlap and New York event windows, then connects gold to conditional macro drivers such as the dollar, rates, inflation expectations, policy, risk and geopolitics without reducing those relationships to mechanical correlations.
