Technical Analysis & Price Action Trading Books

Technical analysis becomes useful when it gives a trader a repeatable language for describing what price has already done and what would invalidate a current idea. This collection focuses on price action, candlesticks, support and resistance, breakouts, Wyckoff structure, Fibonacci, confluence and related chart-reading skills. It is designed for readers who want a stronger foundation before moving into specialized tools such as order flow, options or algorithmic systems. The emphasis is on reading structure clearly, separating observation from prediction, and connecting every setup to risk and invalidation.

Beyond the Single Candle

A Visual Field Guide to Inside Bars, Engulfing Patterns, and Two-Candle Price Action

Beyond the Single Candle moves away from memorizing isolated candlestick names and studies what changes from Candle 1 to Candle 2. Range, body and close location are kept as separate dimensions so an Inside Bar, body engulfing pattern or Outside Bar has a mechanical definition before context is interpreted. Compression is treated as an observable condition, not a directional prediction.

BREAKOUT & RETEST TRADING

Breakout and Retest: A Rules-Based Trading Strategy

Breakout & Retest Trading organizes the strategy into a fixed chain: context, level, break, acceptance, retest, trigger, invalidation, target space, risk and execution. The book begins by defining meaningful levels and distinguishing a real break from a brief penetration, then studies closes, acceptance, follow-through, volume, overextension and the quality of the return to the broken area.

A Visual Trading Course for Beginners

Confluence Trading

A Visual Practical Guide to Combining Price Action, Market Structure, Key Levels, Multiple Timeframes, and Risk

Confluence Trading treats confluence as a way to organize evidence, not a score that makes uncertainty disappear. The early chapters distinguish independent evidence from duplicate evidence and put market structure and location before indicators. Key levels, price action, support and resistance, Fibonacci, moving averages, momentum, volume and multiple timeframes are evaluated according to what new information they add rather than how many boxes they allow the trader to tick.

Trading Fakeouts: How to Avoid the Trap

False Breakouts / Fakeouts turns the familiar idea of a failed breakout into an operational sequence that can be recorded before the result is known. The book distinguishes the reference boundary, penetration, acceptance outside the level, reclaim, confirmation and invalidation so a wick beyond support or resistance is not automatically labeled manipulation or reversal.

A Practical Guide to Accumulation, Distribution, Springs, Upthrusts, and Market Structure
