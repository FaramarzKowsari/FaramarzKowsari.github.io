Algorithmic, AI & Systematic Trading Books

Systematic and AI-assisted trading moves discretion into rules, data definitions, models, testing assumptions and production controls. These books cover AI trading agents, trading bots, chatbot-assisted workflows, multi-timeframe evidence, strategy specification and controlled automation. The central standard is reproducibility: an idea should be clear enough to test, costs should be included, leakage and overfitting should be challenged, and automation should never bypass independent risk controls.

AI Trading Agents & AI Trading Bots

Building Smarter Trading Workflows

AI Trading Agents & AI Trading Bots treats an AI trading system as a governed workflow rather than a model attached directly to execution. It follows the path from evidence and market state through models, policy, abstention and independent risk to order-state reconciliation, portfolio constraints, testing, production architecture, security, graded autonomy and five end-to-end builds. The final sections study failure chains, backtest overfitting, uncertainty and controlled deployment, supported by implementation templates, checklists and incident dossiers.

AI-Assisted Crypto Futures

The Smart Three-Screenshot Method for Multi-Timeframe Futures Trading

AI-Assisted Crypto Futures builds a disciplined workflow around three clean screenshots: 1H for direction, 15M for the working zone and 5M for timing. Screenshots are treated as evidence rather than decoration, so each one must show the timeframe, recent swing points, active price area and enough history for the AI assistant to evaluate whether price is extended, balanced or structurally unclear. The prompt must ask for visible evidence, invalidation, risk and a reason to stand aside.
