About

Technology, education and publishing

Faramarz Kowsari is an author, software engineer and AI researcher based in Istanbul. His work explores artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, prompt engineering, financial technology, modern education and personal growth.

He develops open-source educational projects, practical software resources and visual books designed to make complex subjects clearer, more useful and more accessible.
